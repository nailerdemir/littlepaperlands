Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Little Paper Lands Publisher (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts and YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the App uses

  • TikTok account data: when the channel owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and the account's open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), and permission to upload and post videos (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • YouTube (Google) account data: when the channel owner signs in with Google, the App receives OAuth tokens with the scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to confirm which channel it is uploading to, by its channel ID and title).
  • Videos and captions: the channel owner's own video files and captions, which the App uploads to the owner's own TikTok accounts and YouTube channels.

How the information is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts and YouTube channels, and to check that each upload succeeded. The App does not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not show advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user. They are not sent anywhere except to TikTok's and Google's official APIs. A local log records which videos were uploaded, to avoid posting the same video twice.

Sharing

No information is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok and Google, as required to upload videos. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions); the local token files are then deleted.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @littlepaperlands on Instagram.
