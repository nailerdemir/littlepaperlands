Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Little Paper Lands Publisher (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the Little Paper Lands channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Information the App uses

  • TikTok account data: when the channel owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and the account's open ID, display name and username (scope user.info.basic), and permission to upload and post videos (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • Videos and captions: the channel's own video files and captions, which the App uploads to the channel's TikTok account.

How the information is used

Only to upload and publish Little Paper Lands videos to the channel's own TikTok account and to check that the upload succeeded. The App does not collect data about viewers or any other TikTok users, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not show advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user. They are not sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API. A local log records which videos were uploaded, to avoid posting the same video twice.

Sharing

No information is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as required to upload videos.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the local token files are then deleted.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @littlepaperlands on Instagram.
